# Stock Audit Features

## 1. Stock Transfer Verification

**How it works:**  
Audits goods in transit between warehouses, stores, or internal locations by matching dispatch records against physical receipts.

**Relevance to an Inventory Management System:**  
Prevents stock leakage and inventory inflation by ensuring items sent from one location are accurately accounted for and received in the system before balances update.

## 2. Physical Location and Bin-Level Verification

**How it works:**  
Verifies that items are physically stored in their assigned locations, warehouse zones, aisles, or bins.

**Relevance to an Inventory Management System:**  
Prevents "ghost inventory" (stock that exists in system records but cannot be found on the floor), ensuring order fulfillment and picking processes remain accurate and efficient.

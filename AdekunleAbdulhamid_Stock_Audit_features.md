# Stock Audit Features

## 1. Count Sheets and Count Tags

**Explanation:**
A count sheet is a printed or digital list of all items expected to be in stock, with blank spaces for the actual quantity found. A count tag is a numbered tag attached to each item or stock location once it has been counted. Together, they show which items have been counted and which have not.

**Relevance to an Inventory Management System:**
- Gives a record of the physical stock to compare against the quantities in the system.
- Prevents items from being counted twice or skipped.
- Helps reveal shortages, overages, and recording errors.
- Supports accurate stock reports and better reorder decisions.

## 2. Insurance and Safeguarding Check

**Explanation:**
This check confirms that the stock is protected against loss, theft, fire, and damage. It covers whether the insurance policy is valid and covers the stock's current value, and whether physical security measures (locks, restricted access, alarms, safe storage) are in place.

**Relevance to an Inventory Management System:**
- Ensures stock value in the system matches what the insurance covers.
- Reduces losses from theft, damage, and unauthorized access.
- Allows quick claims, because the system holds a record of what was in stock.
- Protects the accuracy and reliability of inventory data over time.

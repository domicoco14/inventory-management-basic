# Stock Audit Features

## 1. Ghost Inventory Detection

**Explanation:**
Ghost inventory is a condition where the inventory management system shows that a product is in stock and available for sale, but the physical shelf or bin has actually been empty for a long period. The system flags this item as "ghost" because it has recorded zero sales, zero movements, and zero physical counts for months despite showing a positive quantity. This situation usually occurs when stock was stolen, damaged, expired, or transferred without being properly adjusted in the system. Most standard inventory systems miss this because they only check current quantity, not the sales velocity and time-since-last-seen.

**Relevance to Inventory Management System:**
It is highly relevant because it directly improves stock accuracy and prevents revenue loss. It helps managers identify hidden shrinkage, avoid promising products to customers that do not exist physically, and ensures that purchase reports and reordering decisions are based on real stock. It also triggers automatic investigations and stock adjustments, which makes the entire system more reliable.

# Stock Audit Features

## 2. Audit Reports

### Short Explanation
An audit report is a written report of what the stock audit found, including any differences between the physical stock and the quantity recorded in the system.

### Relevance to an Inventory Management System
It provides a clear record of the audit results, helps track inventory discrepancies, and allows management to take corrective action when necessary.

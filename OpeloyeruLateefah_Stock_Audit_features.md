# Stock Audit Features

## 2. Photo Evidence Audit

**Explanation:**
Photo Evidence Audit is a mandatory step in the stock audit process where the auditor must capture a clear, timestamped photo of the shelf, bin, or pallet to complete and submit their count. This photo serves as visual proof of what was actually on the shelf at the time of counting. If a variance or stock discrepancy is discovered later, the manager can review the photo to determine whether the item was miscounted during the audit or if it was stolen or moved after the audit was completed. This is particularly useful in a retail or warehouse environment where different staff work in shifts and accountability is needed.

**Relevance to Inventory Management System:**
This feature is critical for accountability, transparency, and audit integrity. It creates a verifiable, non-editable record that reduces human error, prevents fraudulent counts, and resolves disputes between shifts. It also improves trust in the audit data and provides strong evidence for management decisions, loss prevention, and staff performance review.

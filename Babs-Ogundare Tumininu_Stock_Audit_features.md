# Stock Audit Features

## 1. Stock Level Monitoring

**Explanation:**  
Tracking the minimum, maximum, and reorder levels of stock to ensure that the right quantity of items is maintained.

**Relevance to Inventory Management System:**  
It helps the system monitor stock quantities, prevent stock shortages or overstocking, and indicate when items need to be reordered.

## 2. Expiry and Damage Checks

**Explanation:**  
Identifying expired, damaged, or obsolete items in the inventory.

**Relevance to Inventory Management System:**  
It helps the system keep accurate records, remove unusable items, reduce losses, and ensure that only usable stock remains available.

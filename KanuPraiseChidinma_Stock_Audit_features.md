# Stock Audit Features

## 1. Audit Sampling Technique

### Short Explanation
Instead of counting all inventory items, the Inventory Management System (IMS) randomly selects a representative sample of SKUs for auditing. If the variance found in the sample is within the acceptable tolerance level, the system assumes that the overall stock is accurate.

### Relevance to an Inventory Management System
This feature saves time and reduces the effort required for stock audits, especially in large warehouses with many SKUs. It allows the IMS to identify potential inventory discrepancies efficiently without requiring a complete physical count of all stock.

## 2. Work-in-Progress and Raw Material Verification

### Short Explanation
This feature audits raw materials and unfinished goods on the production floor, rather than focusing only on finished products. It verifies the quantity of materials issued against the quantity actually consumed during production to detect wastage, shortages, or diversion.

### Relevance to an Inventory Management System
This feature helps the IMS maintain accurate records of raw materials and work-in-progress inventory. It improves production accountability, helps identify material losses, and ensures that inventory usage matches production records.

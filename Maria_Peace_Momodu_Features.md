# Stock Audit Features

## 1. Consignment/Third-Party Stock Verification

**Short Explanation:**  
This feature involves checking and verifying stock that is kept with or owned by outside parties, such as suppliers, distributors, or agents.

**Relevance to an Inventory Management System:**  
It helps the system keep accurate records of stock held by third parties and ensures that the quantity and ownership of such stock are properly tracked and verified.

## 2. Item Code and Unit of Measure Checks

**Short Explanation:**  
This feature ensures that every inventory item has the correct item code and the appropriate unit of measure, such as pieces, boxes, or cartons.

**Relevance to an Inventory Management System:**  
It helps prevent errors when recording, tracking, purchasing, or issuing stock, ensuring that inventory quantities are accurate and consistent throughout the system.

# Stock Audit Features

## 1. Segregation of Duties

The person responsible for counting the physical stock should be different from the person responsible for recording the inventory. This separation of responsibilities helps reduce errors, fraud, and manipulation during the stock auditing process.

### Relevance to an Inventory Management System

This feature improves accountability and accuracy by ensuring that inventory counts are independently verified. It reduces the possibility of one person manipulating both the physical count and the recorded inventory figures.

## 2. Stock Cut-Off Procedures

Stock movements should be temporarily stopped during the physical stock count. This ensures that items are not being added, removed, sold, or transferred while the inventory is being counted.

### Relevance to an Inventory Management System

This feature ensures that the physical stock count corresponds with the inventory records at a specific point in time. It helps prevent discrepancies caused by stock movements occurring during the counting process.

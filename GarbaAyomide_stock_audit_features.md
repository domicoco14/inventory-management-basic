# Stock Audit Features

## 1. Stock Write-Off Documentation

Stock write-off documentation is the process of keeping detailed records of items that are removed from the inventory because they are damaged, expired, lost, stolen, or no longer usable or sellable.

## 2. Obsolete, Slow-Moving, or Damaged Stock Identification

This involves identifying inventory that has not been sold or used for a long period, items that are becoming outdated, or goods that have been damaged and can no longer be sold normally.

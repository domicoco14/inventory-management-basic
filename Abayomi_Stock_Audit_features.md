# Stock Audit Features

## 1. Predictive Anomaly Detection with Auto-Triggered Audits

### Short Explanation
This feature uses data analysis to identify unusual stock movements, such as unexpected shortages, excessive stock adjustments, or unusual sales patterns. When an anomaly is detected, the system can automatically trigger an audit for further investigation.

### Relevance to an Inventory Management System
It helps an inventory management system detect potential errors, fraud, or stock discrepancies early. This improves stock accuracy, reduces losses, and allows inventory managers to investigate problems before they become serious.

## 2. Tamper-Evident Ledger with Automated Root-Cause Tracing

### Short Explanation
This feature keeps a secure record of inventory transactions and changes. The records are designed to show when information has been altered, while automated tracing helps identify the transaction or action that caused a stock discrepancy.

### Relevance to an Inventory Management System
It improves accountability and transparency by maintaining a reliable history of stock activities. It also makes it easier for managers to trace inventory problems back to their source and take appropriate corrective action.

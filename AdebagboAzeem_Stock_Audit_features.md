# Stock Audit Features

## 1. Batch/Serial Number Tracking

Batch/Serial Number Tracking is the process of identifying and tracking inventory items using their unique batch or serial numbers.

### Relevance to Inventory Management

It helps the inventory management system keep track of specific products, making it easier to trace items, monitor their movement, and identify particular batches or individual products when necessary.

## 2. Stock Valuation

Stock Valuation is the process of determining the monetary value of the inventory held by a business.

### Relevance to Inventory Management

It helps the inventory management system calculate the financial value of available stock, allowing the business to monitor the worth of its inventory and maintain accurate financial records.

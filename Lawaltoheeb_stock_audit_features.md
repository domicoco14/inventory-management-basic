# Stock Audit Features

## 1. FIFO/FEFO Checks

**Explanation:**  
FIFO (First-In, First-Out) ensures that older stock is sold or used before newer stock. FEFO (First-Expired, First-Out) ensures that products with the nearest expiration dates are used or sold first.

**Relevance to an Inventory Management System:**  
This feature helps the system track stock age and expiration dates, reducing expired products, waste, and losses.

## 2. Supplier/Receiving Verification

**Explanation:**  
This feature checks whether the goods delivered by a supplier match the quantity and items that were originally ordered.

**Relevance to an Inventory Management System:**  
It helps maintain accurate stock records by ensuring that only the correct products and quantities received are added to the inventory.
